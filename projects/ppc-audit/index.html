Advertising audit copilot

A three-level Cowork workspace that frames an assistant to audit Amazon ad campaigns with method and guardrails.

Category

TechnologiesClaude Cowork, Skills, Plugins, Markdown

The project

Each client and each brand has its own Cowork project, reading a shared methodology in read-only mode. The assistant runs a four-block audit and gives no figures until five baseline inputs are gathered.

What I did with AI

  • A context architecture

    A single instruction template serves every project unchanged. The methodology separates safe formulas from the assistant's opinions.

  • An onboarding skill

    A Cowork plugin asks for the product structure, then creates a new brand's whole folder tree.

  • Against sycophancy

    Each of the assistant's positions has a reason and review conditions. It must cite its position before changing it.

What was hard

The AI that gave in too fast

The problem
The assistant changed its mind as soon as someone pushed back.
What I did
A file of tracked positions, each with its reason and review conditions.
The result
A change of mind must now be justified by new data.

The change that did not stick

The problem
A bid reduction made in the interface reverted on its own within the day.
What I did
A verification protocol after every action.
The result
Systematic checks at 5 minutes, 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days.
