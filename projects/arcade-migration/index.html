The project

Inventory of a 1 TB+ disk, a migration plan, scripted clean-up of a 100,000+ file catalogue, system settings and restoration on a new machine.

What I did with AI

  • An agent that acts, under rules

    Claude Code runs commands itself over SSH. It moves, never deletes, and each script runs as a dry run until given `--go`. The final clean-up ran from its lists, reviewed and launched by me.

  • A numbered-file protocol

    Briefs and reports are exchanged as numbered files, from 001 to 077. The log is the source of truth after every compaction.

What was hard

Two wrong hypotheses

The problem
Entries showed their neighbour's details. I blamed the database, the agent blamed parallel requests.
What I did
Re-querying by fingerprint, then reading the title stored in the files themselves.
The result
The real cause: files misnamed at the source. 24 pairs fixed.
