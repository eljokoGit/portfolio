The project

An open question: which ventures could come out of my skills? Rather than asking one model for an opinion, I set up a four-round study where every idea must survive three sceptics trying to refute it, with sources.

What I did with AI

  • Deterministic workflows

    Scripts orchestrate the agents as a pipeline: each idea moves to review as soon as it is ready, without waiting for the others.

  • Three sceptical lenses

    Market, constraints and feasibility. Each sceptic must refute by default without evidence, and correct the figures with sources.

  • Calibration on real data

    Before any idea, agents established real median revenue per category, to judge every promise against market reality.

  • Resuming after failure

    When a round failed on a rate limit, the workflow journal made it possible to resume without rerunning finished agents.
