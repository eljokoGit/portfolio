TechnologiesPython, Pillow, numpy, ImageCms, SHA-256

The project

Two photo backups had to be merged without duplicates, even though the file names did not match. The AI compared image content, including rotated or mirrored images, and converted HEIC to JPEG without visible loss.

What I did with AI

  • A 4-stage comparison chain

    Size and md5, a 32×32 fingerprint across 8 transformations, 192×192 confirmation, a visual check.

  • Colour measured, not assumed

    Display P3 to sRGB conversion with an embedded profile: ignoring it shifted 18.8% of pixels. All 58 EXIF tags are preserved.

  • A method turned into a skill

    The approach is packaged in an 8-section Claude skill, reusable for any photo backup merge.
