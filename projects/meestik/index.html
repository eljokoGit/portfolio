Meestik

An esoteric-themed dating app, built on a sourced knowledge base written by ten agents, with ethical guardrails written into the code.

Category
Apps
Period
July 2026
Status
Working prototype, knowledge base pending expert review

TechnologiesReact, Vite, TypeScript, Express, astronomy-engine

The project

Each member gets a full profile: astrology, numerology, Chinese astrology and tarot. For each match, the app produces a cross reading of the pair: strengths, points of attention, exercises and a first-date idea.

The founding choice is ethical: no score, and no pair is ever presented as doomed. The knowledge base is the source of truth, and the code enforces it.

What I did with AI

  • A knowledge base written by ten agents

    Ten parallel sub-agents, one per system and one for app benchmarking: 102 web searches, 121 cited sources, 41,378 words, written in 13 minutes.

  • An AI that says what it does not know

    Every sheet carries an "expert review pending" status. Rather than deciding silently, the AI grouped 18 questions for the expert, 7 of them blocking.

  • Ethics in the types

    The score field is typed to always be `null`. Points of attention are capped at half the strengths, and each piece of advice cites the system it comes from.

  • A jury of AI designers

    A workflow produced 3 art directions in parallel, scored by 2 AI judges, one on brand, one on usability. The winner scored 87 out of 100 with both.
